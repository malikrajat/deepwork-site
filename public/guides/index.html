Guides to focused work

Eight pieces about the method, the planning and the data. They are written to be useful before you install anything — and one of them argues that you may not need an app at all.

Why these exist

A product page can tell you what an app does. It is a poor place to learn how to run a day, because the answer is mostly not about the app: it is about choosing one thing, deciding when to stop, and knowing where the hours went. These guides are the long answer to the questions the front page can only summarise.

Each one stands alone. Where DeepWork is relevant, it is named at the end as one way to do what the guide describes, with the features that actually exist — not as the only way, and not as a prerequisite for the advice.

How these are written

Every guide is written by Rajat Malik, the person who builds DeepWork. Each one is original to this site: no article is copied, syndicated or rewritten from another publication, and no page here is a reprint of somebody else's work. They are based on the method as it is publicly described, on the behaviour of the application, and on practice — not on a study the page cannot show you. Where a claim would need evidence this site does not have, the guide says what is a convention instead of pretending it is a finding.

There are no sponsored posts, no affiliate links and no paid placements. The guides carry advertising, which is disclosed on each page and described in the cookies page; the advertising has no influence on what is written. If something here is wrong, write to contact@rajatmalik.dev and it will be corrected, with the date at the top of the page changed.

DeepWork is a separate application and these guides are the website's own writing. They are not documentation, and the app is not required to follow any of them.

The product, if you want it

DeepWork is a free, MIT-licensed focus timer, task manager and standing-reminder app that keeps its data on your own machine. The front page describes what it does with the real numbers; these guides work with a paper diary just as well.
