Cookies and your choices

This site stores one preference of its own and loads two Google tags on the page and the guides. Here is every item, who sets it, what it is for, and how to change your mind.

The short version

The DeepWork application stores nothing in a cookie and sends nothing about your work. This website is different only in the ordinary ways a website can be: it counts visits without cookies, it measures page views with Google Analytics, it carries Google AdSense advertising on the guides, and its contact page loads a captcha.

You can accept or refuse advertising and analytics cookies in the regions where consent is required. Refusing does not break the site: the pages read, the guides open and the contact form still works.

What the site itself stores

One key, in localStorage rather than a cookie. It is a file in your browser's own storage, it is never sent with a request, and clearing site data removes it.

Name Set by What it holds Why
deepwork-site-theme This site dark or light So the theme you chose is the theme you get on the next page, without a flash of the other one. Never transmitted.

The visit count is not a cookie either. A pageview is sent by the page itself to GoatCounter, which stores no IP address and sets nothing in your browser. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control are honoured, and the legal documents are not counted at all.

Google Analytics

The front page and the guides load Google Tag Manager, which loads Google Analytics. It sets Google's own analytics cookies to recognise a returning browser and to keep a session together. The common ones are:

Cookie Set by Purpose Typical lifetime
_ga Google Analytics Tells one browser apart from another Up to two years
_ga_<id> Google Analytics Keeps a session's state Up to two years

Google receives the page address, the referring page, your IP address, a description of your browser and device, and an approximate location derived from the request. It processes that under its own privacy policy. The privacy policy, terms, cookies and contact pages do not load the tag.

Advertising cookies

The home page and the guides carry advertising served by Google AdSense. The application itself carries no advertising, and neither do the legal documents or the contact page.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

Third parties may also use web beacons and IP addresses to collect information as a result of ad serving here. Google's explanation is How Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps; the consent rules the site follows are in Google's EU user consent policy.

Cookie Set by Purpose
__gads, __gpi Google Advertising measurement and frequency capping for ads shown on this site
IDE Google (DoubleClick) Recognises a browser so an ad is not shown repeatedly, and measures how an ad performed
__eoi Google Fraud and abuse prevention for advertising

The names and lifetimes are Google's and can change; this table lists the ones the site can be served, not a promise that every one appears on every visit. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings, or opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info.

Nothing on this site is sold. There is no data broker, no audience list and no profile built by this site. Ads are labelled by Google and are not disguised as navigation or content, and nobody is offered a reward for clicking one.

Consent in the EEA, UK, Switzerland and Brazil

In those regions, advertising and analytics storage start denied. Google's consent platform — the certified message configured in AdSense, not a banner built by hand here — asks for a choice and updates the signals when you make one. If you refuse, ads may still appear, but they are not personalised and the advertising cookies described above are not stored. Brazil is covered by Google's LGPD message; the EEA, the UK and Switzerland by its GDPR message.

Outside those regions the defaults are granted, which is why the message does not appear there. You can still refuse with the controls below, and the site will keep working.

The consent signals are Google Consent Mode v2: ad_storage, ad_user_data, ad_personalization and analytics_storage. The defaults are set by /content/consent.js before either Google tag loads, and ads_data_redaction keeps full URLs and identifiers out of Google's advertising signals while storage is denied.

hCaptcha on the contact page

The contact form is protected by hCaptcha (Intuition Machines, Inc.), because a public form without a check fills with spam. hCaptcha sets its own cookies and reads device signals to decide whether the form was filled in by a person. It is loaded only on the contact page, and only when a form endpoint is configured; with JavaScript off, the form falls back to opening your email app and no captcha is loaded.

hCaptcha's handling is described in its privacy policy.

How to change your choice

Four routes, and any one of them is enough:

  1. The consent message. On the page and the guides, Google's consent platform offers a way to reopen your choice. The Cookie settings link in the footer opens it where it is available.
  2. Google Ads Settings. Turn off personalised advertising at adssettings.google.com. This applies to Google across the sites you visit, not only this one.
  3. Industry opt-outs. aboutads.info in the United States and youronlinechoices.eu in Europe.
  4. Your browser. Blocking third-party cookies or using a content blocker stops the advertising and analytics scripts. The site still reads and the guides still open; the only thing lost is the count.

Clearing your browser's site data removes the theme preference and any consent cookie Google set, and you will be asked again where a choice is required.
