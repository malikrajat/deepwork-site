Every screen

Built for the device you are holding

Open DeepWork on a phone, a tablet, a laptop, a desktop monitor or a television browser and you get the same application and the same features. What changes is the layout: it is built around the width it is given, rather than shrunk until it fits.

⌂

No browser chrome

The web app is installable. Once it is on your home screen or your desktop it opens in its own window — its own icon, no address bar, no tab strip. It is the same application; it simply stops looking like a website.

⌁

The same app on the train

The browser build registers a service worker, so it keeps working after the first visit with no connection at all — on a phone, a tablet or a laptop that has gone offline.

◐

It remembers how you left it

Your theme, your session lengths and your preferences are stored on the device and restored before the first paint, so the app does not flash the wrong background at you when it opens on a phone.

One thing that does not change: nothing syncs across these devices. Each browser and each installation keeps its own local database, so the same app on two screens is two separate sets of data — on purpose, and exactly as it is on the desktop. Moving work between them is a CSV export and import, which is written down in the FAQ.
