If you already work this way, skip ahead. If you have heard the word and never been told what it means, this is the whole idea in five steps — and then what DeepWork does with it.

The Pomodoro Technique is a method described by Francesco Cirillo in the late 1980s. DeepWork is an independent implementation of the idea — it is not affiliated with, endorsed by or licensed from the author of the technique.
