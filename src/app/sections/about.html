About the maker

Rajat Malik

Frontend architect and engineering lead — fifteen years of taking large web products apart, putting them back together properly, and staying in the code until they are fast, accessible and boring to release.

I build for enterprise, energy, telecom and finance teams — Siemens since 2020, and Siemens Energy and HCL before that. Most of that work is unglamorous and hard: modernising a legacy Angular or React platform without stopping delivery, drawing module boundaries so two teams can ship in the same week, agreeing the design system once, and moving a Lighthouse score from the wrong side of the dial to the right one.

DeepWork is what I build when nobody is paying me to, and it is the same argument in miniature: local-first by construction, reminders that wait for an answer instead of fading, and a page with no router, no animation library and no third-party script — because a claim that survives being checked is worth more than a stronger one that does not.
