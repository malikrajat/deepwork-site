It is not a calendar event, and not a task

A standing reminder has no quadrant, no project and no completion semantics beyond “handled”, because the things it exists for — a presentation, a class, a dose, a train — are often not work at all.

  • ✓ Done, Snooze 10 min, Stop. The same three answers travel on the desktop notification.
  • ✓ A pause holds it; the mute does not. A pause stops the asking without erasing the reminder, and the overdue one speaks when the pause ends.
  • ✓ No quiet-hours bypass. A reminder respects the pause you set and the quiet hours your OS decides on; it does not override either.
